## 2. Identity and operational representation
Preserve source-local IDs, physical state, compartment, feature, molecular reference, complexes, alternatives, recorded coefficients, and provenance. No automatic merging by gene, chemical class, fingerprint, name, or source citation. State fingerprints and exact event crosswalks are candidates only. 440 multirecord fingerprint groups, 947 records; 344 groups differ in event-role usage. 682 GO activity occurrences exactly crosswalk to 669 BP events; 614 equal nonempty flattened UniProt sets and 68 strict subsets, all cite same event. Do not treat as independent evidence or automatically merge. Gene-to-protein is many-to-many; of 11,682 human BP protein references 11,308 exact unique HGNC mappings, 310 unique parent candidates, 30 ambiguous, 34 no candidate. Targeted human mappings: 429/429 enabler and 43/44 participant concordant unique; B4DWJ1 both empty. P03208 inactive/de-merged, do not choose successors. Feature_exact means exact feature identifier, not independently verified feature type. Complex member annotation not necessarily complete (65/237 GO complex enablers have no parts); do not impute universal composition. Nonprotein and non-UniProt namespaces retained.

Define source-scoped typed incidence matrices B_role[e,r] with binary known recorded participation. Material inputs/outputs, catalyst, positive and negative regulation, template/product, composition, membership, precedence and each RO predicate remain distinct. 16,179 comparable input/output sets and 16,187 catalyst/positive/negative regulator sets exactly agree across BP/SBML; 4,400 explicit comparable coefficients agree; eight template events excluded from material comparison. No implied thermodynamic irreversibility or default coefficient inference from this audit. 10,145 native control records target events directly; 6,871 catalyst pairs, 2,162 positive pairs, 1,111 negative pairs. Preserve source multiplicity for provenance; binary incidence does not multiply duplicate pairs. V1 control-type parsing failure corrected and resolved by full v2 comparison. No flux balance claim.

Composition feasibility uses H/F/U three-valued logic: AND(required components) F if any F, U if no F and any U or no known parts, otherwise H; OR(alternatives) H if any H, U if no H and any U or no known alternatives, otherwise F. Unknown hybrid composition remains U; chemicals taxon-neutral for this diagnostic only. H/F/U does not determine expression, activity, or which molecule is catalytic. Distinguish human machinery responding to foreign ligand, foreign-required complex, foreign enzymatic machinery, and unresolved context. In particular retain OAS viral RNA response even though recorded complex includes foreign RNA. Human-labelled models do not imply every operation is human autonomous.
