# CFA Part 1 mathematical protocol

Version: 0.3.0 — USER-APPROVED INFERENCE DIRECTION; confirmatory implementation not frozen. Date: 2026-11-27.

Part 1A remains frozen at manifests/P1A_SOURCE_LOCK_V1.json, including the exact historical v0.2.0 protocol at its candidate commit. This revision governs downstream inference, not the approved source bytes or representation policy. See P1A_SOURCE_REPRESENTATION_POLICY.md and SOURCE_LICENSE_POLICY.md. Exact model, eligibility, null constraints, assembly algorithm, seeds and statistical thresholds remain open pending feasibility.

## 1. Estimands and limits
Part 1 estimates reproducible organization of curated molecular knowledge. It is label-excluded, not function-blind: curation decisions shape even unlabeled molecular topology. A structural block is a group of operations with related connection patterns; a composite architecture is a supported assembly of complementary operations; a biological function requires independent evidence of an operational outcome. Neither a block, a nested partition, a path nor a strongly connected component alone establishes a function. No physiological flux, activity, causal effect or tissue-level cooperation claim follows from Part 1 alone.
The primary scientific questions are H02 (incremental information beyond existing pathways, degree and shared machinery) and H05 (mechanistically supported complementary assemblies beyond pathway-conditioned expectations). No lineage-expression or perturbation outcomes may condition molecular discovery. Separate these views until catalogue freeze; audit historical knowledge leakage.

## 2. Source roles and biological scope
Reactome native BioPAX is the primary discovery source. GO-CAM remains in the harmonized catalogue, but is not pooled into primary fitting. Reserve demonstrably nonimported GO-CAM assertions for cross-source corroboration; unknown import/evidence status is not independent evidence. Audit publication overlap, source topology, candidate mapping ambiguity and coverage. GO-only and pooled models are secondary sensitivity analyses, not replacements for primary evaluation. Imported Reactome assertions and repeated representations cannot count as independent support.
Retain all source-local records, physical states, compartments, modifications, roles, AND/OR composition, explicit coefficients and provenance. No automatic merge by gene, fingerprint, citation or chemical ID. Human-compatible, host-response, foreign-dependent, unresolved and disease-context strata remain explicit; exact eligibility rule to be registered in 1B. Disease annotation alone is not exclusion. Existing pathway memberships, hierarchy, gene sets and explicit curated precedence are excluded from primary label-excluded architecture fitting; use as comparison metadata, pathway-conditioned baselines/nulls and separately labelled sensitivity views. Do not use pathway-informed selection to claim completely label-excluded inference.

## 3. Native incidence and mechanistic candidate layers
Let I[e,r], O[e,r], C[e,r], Pplus[e,r], Pminus[e,r] denote binary recorded input, output, catalytic and signed regulatory participation. Zero means unrecorded, not experimentally absent. Preserve explicit stoichiometry separately; no imputation or flux inference. Native causal predicates, template/product, composition and context remain distinct. Do not silently treat negative edges as negative random-walk probabilities or pool layer weights.

For exact-state candidates:
 Aflow = O^T D I; Acat = O^T Dcat C;
 Aplus = O^T Dplus Pplus; Aminus = O^T Dminus Pminus.
Diagonals removed for nonself projections only, native self relations retained. D is nonnegative; edges denote possible dependencies, not actual transfer/activity. Compare unweighted, inverse frequency w_e = log((N+1)/(d_e+1)), bridge-multiplicity normalization (1/b_e if b_e>0), and a prespecified currency sensitivity. Here b_e = nout(e)*nin(e) - sum_r O[e,r]*I[e,r]. Degree definition and currency rules must be declared before confirmatory fitting. Do not upweight favored molecular intermediates by biological intuition. Report cross-pathway bridges by molecular frequency/class/context; distinguish unmapped and multiply annotated operations.

Alternative-member compatibility: for recorded compatible realizations R(e), R(f), K_or[e,f]=1 if their intersection is nonempty and molecular state/location constraints are satisfied. Distinguish possible overlap from guaranteed inclusion R(e) subset R(f) when realizations are fully specified. Missing composition/location remains uncertainty. A_or=O^T K_or I (optional weighting must be defined separately). Preserve exact and expanded layers separately; avoid double-counting exact matches in added-only comparisons. Preserve realization witnesses and hierarchy paths, not a global equivalence relation. Context-dependent set membership does not imply arbitrary substitution.
Required-component dependence: K_and[e,f]=1 if e is a recorded required component of f under compatible composition/context. A_component=O^T K_and I is a possible prerequisite, never sufficient transfer of the complete complex. Preserve other required components, coefficient information and explicit assembly/dissociation events. Prefer actual assembly routes when recorded; no invented reactions. Extension to catalytic/regulatory roles requires separate labels and audit. Do not expand every component into a material edge.
Cross-source identity: strict exact ID plus explicitly compatible molecular context, expanded missing-location candidates separate. Neither implies merging; primary discovery does not depend on GO mappings.
Context: recorded operation context U[q,r] can support compatibility U^T Dq U, not causal dependence. Do not infer organelle state, pH, timing, or transport solely from co-location. Use context constraints and separate exploratory layer; quantify broad compartment hub effects and differential coverage for metabolic, signalling, trafficking, structural and host-response mechanisms. Phagocytosis, ECM and barrier function may be poorly represented by material flow alone. Benchmark functional-domain coverage without imposing those functions in discovery.

## 4. Two-level inference
Level 1: infer structural role modules. Prioritize native typed heterogeneous/bipartite incidence and degree-corrected directed block models; compare compatible projections, directed Infomap, Leiden and overlap alternatives. Illustrative count likelihood: Aij^k ~ Poisson(theta_out[i,k]*theta_in[j,k]*Omega^k[z_i,z_j]); appropriate normalization and admissible pairs required. For binary or fractional observations use compatible likelihoods instead; derived edges and layers are dependent. Native role likelihoods must account for structural eligibility; conditional independence is a modelling assumption, not established. Compare description length Sigma = -log P(data|model)+coding_cost only for identical observations and compatible codes; cross-representation comparison uses common held-out targets. No arbitrary layer sum, no mandatory hierarchy, no predetermined number of modules.
Level 2: infer overlapping composite assemblies, not rename blocks as functions. Let Z[i,a]=1(z_i=a), W^k=Z^T A^k Z. W summarizes candidate block interfaces but cannot certify a continuous molecular mechanism: different operations inside an intermediate block may be disconnected. Architecture F=(V,L,I_boundary,O_boundary,Gamma) retains participating operations, typed links, candidate molecular boundaries and contextual/compositional requirements. Enumerate bounded directed paths, branches, feedback and alternative assemblies; require operation-level witnesses with compatible molecular states and explicit required/alternative components. A path witness alone does not establish simultaneous realizability or flux. Unknown requirements are flagged rather than asserted satisfied. Shared structural modules can participate in multiple assemblies.
Evaluate mechanistic support, complementarity, boundary coherence, redundancy and complexity separately. Prefer constrained/multi-objective search over arbitrary weighted scores; e.g. maximize held-out-supported mechanistic connectivity subject to bounded redundancy/size, minimum complementarity and interface feasibility. Thresholds, search space, witness rules and treatment of incomplete information must be registered before confirmatory testing. Do not select visually attractive assemblies or tune on later functional outcomes. Block discovery and assembly selection are separate steps; neither is yet implemented or certified.
Candidate residual interface statistic R_ab=W_ab-E_null(W_ab|degree,pathway,context); standardized Z_ab=R_ab/sd_null(W_ab) only for nonzero variance. If blocks or assemblies were selected from the tested graph, account for selection in the null or evaluate on independent held-out assertions.

## 5. Curation baseline, prediction and nulls
Use Reactome overlapping/hierarchical memberships X[i,p] as benchmark, not primary discovery edges. At specified levels Cij=sum_p X[i,p]X[j,p]. Compare inferred structural partitions and assemblies to curated membership using AMI/NMI only on well-defined hard partitions (NMI=2I(Z;C)/(H(Z)+H(C))), and overlap-aware co-membership/size-aware comparisons for multiply annotated events. Report coverage, unmapped events, levels and matched universes. High agreement is recovery of known organization; low agreement alone is not novelty.
Primary incremental prediction H02: compare degree, role/context, pathway co-membership, gene overlap and shared machinery baseline against architecture-augmented model on identical held-out native assertions and candidate risk sets. Illustrative logit(p0_ij)=alpha+beta_out log(1+kout_i)+beta_in log(1+kin_j)+beta_path Cij+gamma^T xij; logit(p1_ij)=logit(p0_ij)+Omega[z_i,z_j] or frozen assembly features. Degrees, features and membership for temporal evaluation use training-release knowledge only. Fit/calibrate on discovery/development data; evaluate DeltaS=S1-S0 with uncertainty and appropriately matched candidate universe. Unrecorded assertions are not validated negatives; describe prediction of curation under sampling/censoring scheme, not biological truth. Native assertion targets and decoder must be defined; projected-edge recovery alone is not sufficient.
Null ladder: N0 degree/operation size; N1 adds role, molecular class, compartment, source and appropriate eligibility; N2 adds broad pathway membership constraints; N3 finer pathway/evidence structure where feasible. Overlapping memberships require explicit preservation of membership profiles or pathway-conditioned incidence counts; swaps within one top-level pathway alone may violate others. Audit constraint satisfaction, mixing, diversity and effective resolution; overly constrained nulls may be unidentifiable. Do not silently weaken pathway constraints to claim significance. Preserve or block near-duplicates, imported assertions and shared evidence; randomize native incidence before rebuilding projections. Distinguish direct curated links from projected dependencies. No random projected-edge split as primary validation.
H01 basic organization is diagnostic, not primary novelty. H02 and H05 primary; H03 robustness required; H04 hierarchy secondary. H06 temporal extension. Empirical p=(1+#null>=observed)/(B+1) for a valid upper-tail randomization. Repeat relevant search/model selection under null if testing selected statistic on same observations; alternatively freeze on training and test independent holdout. Pilot compute/mixing before setting B and multiple-testing families; specify effect sizes, error control, and minimum attainable p before confirmatory tests. No fixed 999-run commitment. Assess split/merge-aware stability and evidence-block uncertainty; no post hoc threshold relaxation.

## 6. Temporal and cross-source validation
Historical release discovery, distinct development if feasible, later release evaluation. Select release pair/triple after bounded archive/schema/identity feasibility only, document choices before outcome inspection. Existing v98 aggregate exposure makes v98 retrospective, not untouched; a future unexamined release is stronger prospective validation. Historical snapshots are separate versioned extension receipts/manifests and must never overwrite Part 1A lock. No archive acquisition authorized by this protocol revision alone.
Distinguish added assertions among known nodes from new operations, corrections, replacements, splits/merges and deleted assertions. For new nodes require a frozen assignment rule using features disjoint from evaluation targets; otherwise restrict evaluation to known nodes. Use historical pathways, degree, mappings and evidence at training time; avoid later annotations in training. Date of curation is not date of biological discovery. Compare predictive improvement over historical pathway baseline. Report release coverage, publication overlap, lag and exposure.
For nonimported GO-CAM corroboration, freeze Reactome assemblies and correspondence rules before scoring. Example T_GO=mean over eligible mapped GO causal assertions of indicator[their corresponding operations share at least one frozen architecture]. Preserve direction/role, ambiguous mapping and model/publication dependence; compare matched pathway and degree/context baselines, account for assembly overlap and size. Report unmatched assertions; no selection on favorable concordance. Partial corroboration only, not independent experimental validation.

## 7. Gates and catalogue
1B: source-scoped harmonization, identity/compatibility candidates, scope/provenance/pathway benchmark metadata, then representation certification.
1C: native and candidate separate layers; primary Reactome, GO held aside.
1D: actual degree/hub/coverage/domain/pathway diagnostics, null feasibility, bounded comparison grid, leakage-free splits and historical extension feasibility. Freeze candidate models, target definitions, assembly search, tests and stopping criteria before confirmatory analysis; exploratory diagnostics not confirmatory results.
1E: structural role inference and separate operation-witnessed assembly inference. Keep fitted models and search history.
1F: H02/H05 primary pathway-conditioned tests, robustness, temporal extension where feasible, cross-source corroboration. If no feasible valid null or holdout, limit claims.
1G: functional specifications with molecular input/output boundaries, requirements, alternatives, unresolved conditions, comparison to known pathways and uncertainties. No forced naming.
1H: freeze structural modules and composite assemblies separately, membership and interface witnesses, evidence groups, gene mapping, model/graph parameters, pathway correspondence, incremental support, domain coverage, uncertainty and checksums. Do not equate catalogue with validated cellular functions. Only then open expression and functional validation.
Gate failures trigger documented review; no model, numerical threshold or scientific result certified by this amendment.
